Hundreds of full-body emotes and dances for Build 42, ported from FlipMods' TooManyEmotes (Lethal Company).

Every emote is unlocked and reachable from the vanilla emote wheel (Too Many Emotes slice) or from a searchable emote browser with favorites.

Animations are retargeted from the original Unity humanoid clips onto the Project Zomboid skeleton, fingers included.

Original emotes and animations: FlipMods, TooManyEmotes (MIT license).
